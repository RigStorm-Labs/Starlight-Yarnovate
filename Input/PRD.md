**## 4. Design System — Rustic Yarn Studio**



**### Design Direction**

**Natural, warm, artisanal, elegant, and professional. The website should feel like a contemporary crochet studio, not a generic corporate template.**



**### Color Palette**



**| Color | Hex | Usage |**

**|---|---|---|**

**| Warm Ivory | `#F6F0E5` | Main backgrounds |**

**| Oatmeal | `#D9C5A5` | Secondary surfaces |**

**| Terracotta | `#B96F4A` | Primary accents and CTAs |**

**| Muted Olive | `#59604A` | Secondary accents |**

**| Deep Brown | `#342F29` | Main text |**

**| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and contrast surfaces |**



**Use warm ivory as the dominant background, deep brown for readable text, and terracotta and olive as restrained accents. Verify text contrast before finalizing buttons.**



**### Typography**

**- \*\*Headings:\*\* Elegant, readable serif such as Lora.**

**- \*\*Body:\*\* Clean sans-serif such as Inter.**

**- Maintain clear hierarchy, comfortable line-height, and responsive text sizing.**

**- Provide reliable fallback fonts.**



**### Visual Style**

**- Generous whitespace.**

**- Refined layouts with clear content hierarchy.**

**- Soft, subtle borders and shadows.**

**- Gently rounded buttons and cards.**

**- Authentic crochet photography and natural textures.**

**- Minimal craft-inspired decoration.**

**- No excessive gradients, neon colors, glassmorphism, or distracting animations.**



**### Photography**

**Prefer genuine crochet creations, yarn, crochet hooks, and handmade details photographed in natural light.**



**Use clearly identified placeholders until real images are supplied. Keep image files easy to replace.**

**## 8. SEO, Accessibility, and Performance**



**### SEO**

**- Unique page title and meta description for each page.**

**- Correct viewport metadata.**

**- Appropriate favicon and social-sharing metadata.**

**- Descriptive headings and image alt text.**

**- Sitemap and robots.txt where appropriate.**

**- Verify the final canonical URL after domain configuration.**

**- Do not add fake reviews or unsupported structured data.**



**### Accessibility**

**- Semantic HTML and logical heading hierarchy.**

**- Keyboard-accessible navigation.**

**- Visible focus indicators.**

**- Sufficient color contrast.**

**- Descriptive link labels.**

**- Appropriate image alt text.**

**- Usable layouts at increased zoom.**

**- Respect reduced-motion settings.**



**### Performance**

**- Optimize images.**

**- Avoid unnecessary libraries and scripts.**

**- Lazy-load below-the-fold images.**

**- Avoid delaying the hero image unnecessarily.**

**- Minimize layout shifts.**

**- Ensure the core content and links work without JavaScript where practical.**
